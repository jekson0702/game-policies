# Privacy policy for Quiet Mowing

Android game Quiet Mowing / «Тихий покос». Revision date: 16 November 2026.

Russian version: [privacy.html](privacy.html).

## 1. Developer

Yakavets Yauheni, an individual in Belarus. Questions about data and support: [yakavets.yauheni@gmail.com](mailto:yakavets.yauheni@gmail.com).

## 2. Game data

The game does not require registration and does not ask for the player’s name, phone number, email, photos, contacts, or location. The developer has no own server for accounts or saves.

Progress, purchases made with in-game currency, achievements, language and sound settings, and confirmations of rewarded ads are stored in the app’s private storage on the device. The developer does not receive them automatically. There is no sync with other devices or with Yandex Games cloud saves. System backup of the game files is turned off in the app settings.

## 3. Advertising

The game uses optional rewarded ads from the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, including the AppMetrica components that SDK requires. The player chooses to watch an ad to receive a bonus or unlock a theme. The SDK may start and load ads in advance when the game opens. The main game can be played without an internet connection.

To serve ads, the SDK may process technical details about the device and the app, the Android version, network details including the IP address, service identifiers used by the SDK, and events for ad loading, display, and interaction. The developer does not send the player’s balance or mowed areas to the advertising SDK.

Age-restricted data collection is enabled for every player, location tracking is off, and consent for personalized ads is not granted. The Android location permissions and the advertising-ID permission `AD_ID` are not present. Technical communication with the ad provider still takes place.

Yandex describes its processing and retention in the [Yandex privacy policy](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/). Created using the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK.

## 4. Children

The game is intended for a general audience, including children, and is not positioned exclusively as a children’s game. It does not ask for a child’s age or personal details. The advertising SDK’s age-restricted mode applies to every player. A parent or legal guardian can contact the developer about a child’s data.

## 5. Storage and deletion

Local data remains until progress is reset, the app’s data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Resetting inside the game keeps language and sound settings; clearing the app’s data in Android deletes those settings as well. The ad provider deletes its data under its own rules — uninstalling the game does not delete that data automatically.

If you write to support, the developer receives the sender’s address, the message, and any attachments. They are used to reply and are kept while needed to handle the request and its follow-up, then deleted unless the law requires longer storage. You can ask for the correspondence to be deleted, or ask for information about the processing, at the email address above. Mail is handled through Gmail, which is also covered by the [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

## 6. Changes and contact

When the policy changes, the new revision is published at this same address with a new date. The public page is hosted on GitHub Pages; technical requests to that page are processed under the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). These third-party services may process data outside the user’s country.

For requests to access, correct, or delete data, and to stop processing where applicable: [yakavets.yauheni@gmail.com](mailto:yakavets.yauheni@gmail.com). For data held by the ad provider, you can also contact Yandex under its policy.
